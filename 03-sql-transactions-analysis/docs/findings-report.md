# Findings Report — Transactions Analysis
## NovaBank Instant Transfers

> **Fictitious data, for demonstration purposes only.**

## 1. Scope and method

- **Source:** `data/novabank.db` (SQLite), generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`.
- **Volume:** 3,000 customers · 5,000 accounts · 50,000 transfers · 6 channels · 180 days.
- **Queries:** 15 commented SQL scripts in [`../sql/`](../sql/), executed with `scripts/run_queries.py`.
- **Caveat:** the dataset is synthetic and designed to be realistic, not representative of a real bank. Figures below come from this dataset only.

## 3. Key findings

### F1 — Instant transfers met the speed objective
Completed instant transfers settle in an average of **5 seconds (2–9 s range)**,
with **100% under the 10-second target** (query 05). This satisfies the
`< 10 s` objective for the feature (OBJ-01) within this dataset.

### F2 — Instant adoption is healthy but transfers smaller tickets
Instant transfers are **55.2% of volume** but only **20.1% of value**
(4.83M vs 16.09M, query 04). The average instant ticket is **200.62** versus
**822.76** for batched transfers — roughly **4x smaller** (query 04). Adoption
is strong, but each instant payment moves much less value, which pressures
fee revenue if instant transfers are free.

### F3 — ~1 in 8 transfers does not complete
Only **87.22%** of transfers reach "Completed". The remaining **12.78%** splits
into Pending **4.87%**, Rejected **3.90%**, Returned **2.04%** and Held for
review **1.97%** (query 06).

### F4 — The single biggest failure bucket is pending/timeout handling
**2,434 payments are "Pending"**, of which **1,234 are "Rail timeout"** and
**1,200 "Awaiting reconciliation"** (query 07). This is the largest non-settled
category and directly matches the UAT defect found in
[Project 2, DEF-001](../../02-uat-test-plan/docs/test-results-report.md)
(timed-out transfers not transitioning to a terminal/Pending state cleanly).

### F5 — Rejections are spread across four main causes
Card-style rejections (query 07): **Exceeds daily limit 501**, **Insufficient
available balance 492**, **Beneficiary account closed 484**, **Sanctions
screening match 475**. Returns split between **Beneficiary account not found
(524)** and **Receiving bank returned funds (497)**.

### F6 — Fraud engine flags 2% of transfers — but 42% of flagged value settled
**1,011 transfers (2.02%)** are flagged suspicious, totalling **491,949.17**
(query 08). Of these, **420 flagged transfers worth 202,464.03 were Completed**
(potential fraud exposure), while **477 worth 217,165.14 were held**
(protected). Flagged activity concentrates in **Mobile App (525 of 1,011,
~52%)**, followed by Online Banking (258). The high share of *completed*
high-score transfers points to a screening gap — consistent with the blocked
high-risk test **DEF-003** in Project 2.

### F7 — Mobile App dominates volume; Open API has the weakest success rate
| Channel | Transfers | Value | Success |
|---------|:---------:|------:|:-------:|
| Mobile App | 26,031 | 12,469,579.71 | 87.4% |
| Online Banking | 12,891 | 6,100,627.28 | 87.1% |
| ATM | 4,004 | 1,918,907.03 | 87.3% |
| Branch | 3,047 | 1,513,472.82 | 86.9% |
| Open API / Partner | 2,513 | 1,199,177.24 | **86.5%** |
| Contact Centre | 1,514 | 747,792.53 | 87.1% |

Digital channels (Mobile App + Online Banking + Open API) carry **~83% of
value** (query 03). Open API / Partner has the lowest success rate, suggesting
an integration to investigate.

### F8 — 19% of customers have never transacted
By recency of last transaction (query 11): **74.7% active (≤30 days)**,
**5.9% at risk (31–90 days)**, **0.3% dormant (>90 days)** and
**19.0% (571 customers) with no activity at all** — onboarded but transactional
silence. Monthly active customers are stable at ~2,200 after the partial first
month (query 10).

### F9 — Retail drives volume; Premium has the highest ticket
| Segment | Customers | Transfers | Value | Avg ticket |
|---------|:---------:|:---------:|------:|:----------:|
| Retail | 1,962 | 40,287 | 19,269,101.04 | 478.30 |
| Premium | 357 | 7,252 | 3,513,874.98 | **484.54** |
| SME | 110 | 2,461 | 1,166,580.59 | 474.03 |

Retail accounts for **~80% of value**; Premium shows the highest average ticket
(query 12). The top 15 customers by value range from 36.9k to **64.3k**
(query 13).

### F10 — Internal (on-us) transfers lead external by ~2:1
**32,638 internal (65%)** vs **17,362 external (35%)** transfers, with similar
success rates (87.1% vs 87.4%, query 15).

## 4. Recommendations

| ID | Recommendation | Linked finding | Expected impact |
|----|----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| R1 | Fix the timeout/reconciliation flow so timed-out transfers move to a clear terminal state and notify the customer. | F4 | Reduce the 2,434 pending transfers and "where is my transfer?" contacts (OBJ-02). |
| R2 | Investigate the 420 flagged-but-completed transfers (202k exposure) and tighten real-time hold rules. | F6 | Lower fraud loss ratio toward the ≤ 0.02% target (OBJ-04). |
| R3 | Launch an activation campaign for the 571 never-active customers. | F8 | Increase MAU and instant-transfer adoption (OBJ-05). |
| R4 | Re-engage the 178 at-risk and 10 dormant customers with targeted offers. | F8 | Improve retention and value per customer. |
| R5 | Add pre-submission validation in the app (daily-limit and balance checks) before the transfer is sent. | F5 | Cut "exceeds daily limit" (501) and "insufficient balance" (492) rejections. |
| R6 | Promote instant transfers while monitoring ticket size; consider a tiered fee for larger instant amounts. | F2 | Grow adoption without eroding fee revenue. |
| R7 | Review the Open API / Partner integration (lowest success rate). | F7 | Recover partner-channel completion rate. |
