# Project 1 — Requirements: Instant Transfers (NovaBank)

> **Fictitious data, for demonstration purposes only.**
> NovaBank is an invented bank. All requirements, stakeholders and figures in
> this project are fictional and created solely to demonstrate Business
> Analysis skills.

## Overview

This project documents the requirements for **Instant Transfers**, a new
real-time 24/7 payment feature for NovaBank retail customers. It is the entry
point of a five-project case study that follows the feature through
requirements, UAT, data analysis, process improvement and KPI reporting.

## Key facts

- **Feature:** Instant Transfers (real-time retail payments, 24/7).
- **In scope:** initiating, validating, executing, tracking and notifying instant transfers between NovaBank accounts and to other banks.
- **Out of scope:** corporate/bulk payments, cross-border remittances, credit products.
- **Primary stakeholders:** Retail Customers, Payments Operations, Fraud & Risk, Compliance, IT/Engineering, Customer Support.

## Evidence of skills

- Business analysis: BRD authoring, scope definition, business rules, risk assessment.
- Requirements engineering: user stories and behaviour-driven acceptance criteria (Gherkin).
- Process modelling: Mermaid AS-IS/TO-BE diagrams.
- Governance: RACI and end-to-end traceability to UAT.
